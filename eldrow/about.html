About eldrow

eldrow is the daily five-letter word game, played backwards.

Every day there's one new puzzle, the same for everyone. The answer is always a real word, but spelled in reverse: if the word of the day is PUNCH, you're looking for HCNUP. You have six guesses. Each guess can be any real five-letter word, forwards or backwards, and the tiles turn green and yellow to show how close you got.

Everything else is reversed too, so if you win, don't be surprised to see !didnelpS.

A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight, your local time. Your progress and streaks are saved in your browser.
